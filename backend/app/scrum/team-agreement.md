# Part 2 — Team Agreement

## 1. Team Values

Communication, accountability, and respect will be our top priorities. It is important for our team to communicate often and effectively to prevent any misunderstandings.

## 2. Meetings

We will try to meet once a week, but at a minimum once every two weeks.

Aside from labs, we will meet at designated meeting spots like the library study/meeting rooms. The time would be every other Thursday around 3 p.m.; however, this can change depending on changes in schedule. We can meet more or less often as needed.

If someone cannot attend, the meeting can be rescheduled upon the agreement of everyone. We can also switch the meeting to Zoom.

## 3. Communication

We will use Discord, Gmail, and Messages to communicate with each other.

For urgent matters, Messages will be used.

Important information will be shared through Gmail or Discord.

## 4. Response Expectations

Team members should respond within 24 hours to messages.

If someone cannot respond, they should respond as soon as they can and inform the group beforehand if they know they will be unable to respond for a specific amount of time. They should also inform the group of the reason whenever they are able to respond.

If a member repeatedly fails to respond, we will have a group meeting to inquire about the issue.

## 5. Respect & Professional Conduct

Respectful behaviour within the team means not discriminating against others based on race, ethnicity, or identity and treating everyone with fairness and kindness despite any differences in views or perspectives.

It is also important to include everyone in conversations and ensure everyone's insights, thoughts, and perspectives regarding the project are considered.

Discriminatory behaviour, rude or impolite attitudes, excluding others, avoiding assigned work, and continuously ignoring messages without notifying the team are unacceptable.

Tasks will be assigned equally to everyone, and everyone will have the opportunity to provide their input and explain the tasks they completed during meetings. This way, everyone will have the opportunity to participate.

## 6. Accountability

If any of the expectations in this agreement are not met by a team member, then after a team meeting, the issue will be brought to the TA to resolve.

## 7. Decision-Making

The team will make decisions through majority voting because it is a simple and uncomplicated decision-making option.

If a team member disagrees, they can give the rest of the team their input and another vote can occur. If the vote does not change, then the team will proceed with the majority decision.

## 8. Conflict Resolution

If there is a team disagreement—for instance, if voting is 50/50—or there are other issues regarding the setup, design, execution, or implementation of the project, we will first attempt to resolve the issue through communication and team meetings.

If the issue cannot be resolved, the TA will be informed of the disagreement and asked about the next steps the team should take.

If this does not resolve the issue, the instructor will be informed.

## 9. Anticipated Challenges

Here are some potential challenges and situations the team may face and how they will be addressed:

- **Missed meetings:** Inform the group as soon as possible and get updated on what was missed during the meeting.
- **Missed deadlines:** Inform the group as soon as possible so the issue can be resolved by reducing the number of tasks assigned to that member and distributing necessary work to the rest of the team so the project is not negatively affected.
- **Unequal contributions:** Redistribute future work so the workload of team members who are doing more work is reduced.
- **Poor communication:** Have a group meeting with all members to discuss the issue or send a private message to the team member to understand why they are not responding. Everyone should also contribute their thoughts on the project and inform other team members of what they changed, updated, or added and why.
- **Scheduling conflicts:** Communicate and change the meeting time. If someone will be unable to finish their work within the required time, redistribute the work and try to resolve the issue respectfully.
- **Disagreements between team members:** If the issue cannot be resolved through communication and a team meeting, inform the TA during the lab or, if it is urgent, through Gmail.
